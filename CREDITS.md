## Inspiration

The rogue-like node-map + NBT-room structure was studied from
`锻造之旅—无尽征途0.32.zip` (Forge 1.20.1, author hp). No scripts, NBT rooms,
textures, FancyMenu configs or custom jars from that pack are copied into this
project; only the architectural idea is reused.

Third-party mods are not redistributed in this repository. The release manifest
references their official download pages.
